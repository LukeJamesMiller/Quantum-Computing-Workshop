# UMKC Quantum Computing Workshop

A one-day, hands-on introduction to quantum computing for newcomers, and a warm-up for the AI for Quantum track at Hack-A-Roo.

**Saturday, October 24, 2026 · 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM · Flarsheim Hall, UMKC**

The doors from the parking garage into Royal Hall are generally open, and you can walk to Flarsheim inside the buildings. Watch for an email Saturday morning if anything needs special instructions. The schedule is subject to minor adjustment.

## Before you arrive

- Bring your laptop.
- Sign up for a free account at [kaggle.com](https://www.kaggle.com) or [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com). We will run every notebook in the browser.
- If you have a local Jupyter installation, that is fine too, but we will not have time to sort out dependencies.
