GETTING STARTED — IN ORDER

If you're just curious what quantum computing actually is and why it matters, start here:

1. IBM Quantum Business Foundations (free, ~1–2 hours)
https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/courses/quantum-business-foundations
A non-technical introduction to the why — what quantum computers are good for now, and what we're working toward.

2. Quantum Country — Michael Nielsen (free, ~2–3 hours)
http://quantum.country
A beautifully written interactive essay that builds real intuition. Uses spaced repetition to help the ideas stick. This is the one I recommend to everyone regardless of background.

If you want to go deeper into the math and theory:

3. Quantum Computing Lecture Notes — Ronald de Wolf (free PDF)
https://homepages.cwi.nl/~rdewolf/qcnotes.pdf
The text I'd build a course around. Rigorous but accessible.

4. Foundations of Quantum Information — John Watrous (free, self-paced)
https://quantum.cloud.ibm.com/learning/en/courses/foundations-of-quantum-information
For anyone who wants to go seriously deep — this is the real thing.

5. Quantum Computation and Quantum Information — Nielsen & Chuang (free PDF)
https://profmcruz.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/quantum-computation-and-quantum-information-nielsen-chuang.pdf
The field's standard textbook. Dense but comprehensive — treat it as a reference once you've built some foundation.

Bonus — Theory of Quantum Information — John Watrous (free PDF)
https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~watrous/TQI/TQI.pdf
A more advanced companion to Nielsen & Chuang.

 QISKIT FALL FEST LECTURE SERIES

I recently gave a week-long series of lectures through Qiskit Fall Fest. The lectures build on each other, so if you're starting from scratch I'd recommend beginning at the beginning — but each one is kept short (~30 minutes) with an accompanying hands-on notebook, so it's easy to work through at your own pace. Topics covered include quantum circuits, Qiskit workflows, quantum algorithms (Shor's, Grover's, Deutsch-Jozsa), error correction, hardware optimization, variational methods (VQE/QAOA), noise mitigation, and quantum careers.
